Dip-buy mean reversion trading bot operating on CBBTC 1-minute bars with $1000 paper capital. The strategy identifies oversold conditions by buying when the price drops by at least 1% from the 120-bar high and shows a bounce off the 10-bar low. Positions are managed with a 180 bps take-profit target or a 150 bps trailing stop, enforced by a 60-bar cooldown period after exits.

## Run it

